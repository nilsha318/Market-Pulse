# AI change log

Two to three entries per person over the course. We grade your understanding and your check, not the number of prompts.

## Entry 1

- **Date:**
- **Goal:** What did you want to achieve?
- **Instruction:** Prompt, skill and context you gave the agent (copy the prompt).
- **Result:** What did the agent deliver? (files, lines, answer)
- **Check:** How did you verify it? What did you look at, run or recalculate by hand?
- **Correction:** What did you change, and why?
- **Reference:** Commit hash or file and line.
